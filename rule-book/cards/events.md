# Event cards

Source: `CARTAS SUCESOS.pdf`. How events enter play: [`round.2`–`round.7`](../rulebook.md#round). How they resolve: [`event.1`, `event.2`](../rulebook.md#events).

"PRESENT pile" and "FUTURE pile" (ES *mazo "Presente"* / *mazo "Futuro"*) are the cards stacked on the PRESENT and FUTURE slots. "Takes a Revolt" follows [`revolt.1`](../rulebook.md#revolt).

## General events

28 cards, one of each. They form the shared event deck ([`setup.6`](../rulebook.md#setup)).

| #  | ID                              | Name (ES)                  | Name (EN)                  | Effect                                                                                               |
| -- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | `event.full-moon`               | Noche de luna llena        | Full Moon                  | Every player may move one troop.                                                                     |
| 2  | `event.mercenaries`             | Mercenarios                | Mercenaries                | Every player may recruit a soldier in a region adjacent to one of their settlements.                 |
| 3  | `event.good-harvest`            | Buena cosecha              | Good Harvest               | Every player may build or upgrade a settlement.                                                      |
| 4  | `event.troubled-times`          | Tiempos revueltos          | Troubled Times             | Shuffle the FUTURE pile.                                                                             |
| 5  | `event.fair-winds`              | Vientos favorables         | Fair Winds                 | Every player gets one extra action on the next action card they play.                                |
| 6  | `event.famine`                  | Hambruna                   | Famine                     | The next recruit card is not played.                                                                 |
| 7  | `event.war-drums`               | Tambores de guerra         | War Drums                  | Every pact is cancelled.                                                                             |
| 8  | `event.earthquake`              | Terremoto                  | Earthquake                 | Every wall is destroyed.                                                                             |

| 26 | `event.patronage`               | Patrocinio del talento     | Patronage                  | The first player may recruit or replace a civilian.                                                  |

Notes:

- `event.order-of-chivalry` and `event.arms-supply` allow a knight or an artillery in a village, which [`recruit.troop.4`–`5`](../rulebook.md#recruit) normally forbid. The event wins.
- Open questions: [`q.event-last-player`](../open-questions.md#qevent-last-player) (Material Shortage), [`q.event-replace`](../open-questions.md#qevent-replace) (Patronage, Prosperity), [`q.event-next-card`](../open-questions.md#qevent-next-card) (Famine, Truce, Fair Winds, Embargo, Chaos).

## Colour-aimed events

Each card names one colour (COLOUR). Players hold them in hand and play them against that colour ([`setup.7`](../rulebook.md#setup)).

### Standard set

| # | ID                        | Name (ES)    | Name (EN)     | Effect                                                                          |
| - | ------------------------- | ------------ | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | `event.assassination`     | Atentado     | Assassination | Player COLOUR loses all their walls and kingdom improvements.                   |
| 2 | `event.corruption`        | Corrupción   | Corruption    | Player COLOUR loses all their civilians and does not play their next action card. |
| 3 | `event.insurrection`      | Insurrección | Insurrection  | Player COLOUR loses all their troops on settlements and takes a Revolt.         |

### "1 JUGADOR (4p)" set

The PDF lists two more cards under the heading *1 JUGADOR (4p)* ("1 player, 4-player game"). The rulebook does not mention them: see [`q.event-4p-set`](../open-questions.md#qevent-4p-set).

| # | ID                          | Name (ES)    | Name (EN)     | Effect                                                                                               |
| - | --------------------------- | ------------ | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1 | `event.insurrection-4p`     | Insurrección | Insurrection  | Player COLOUR takes a Revolt and loses all their walls and civilians.                                |
| 2 | `event.assassination-4p`    | Atentado     | Assassination | Player COLOUR loses all their troops and kingdom improvements, and does not play their next action card. |
